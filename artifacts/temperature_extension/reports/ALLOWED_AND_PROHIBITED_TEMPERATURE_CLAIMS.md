# Allowed and prohibited temperature claims

## Allowed

- Pushed-forward stochasticity increased at T=1.
- Coupling choice affected paired distortion on the fixed official-sampler suite.
- The T=1 macro CRN-minus-lifted mean was positive with its frozen bootstrap interval.
- Effects were heterogeneous and mixed.
- Proposal CMC reduced proposal-vector mismatch.
- Selector compression prevented a universal output-cost ordering.
- All validity, pathwise, and copy-only gates passed.

## Prohibited

- Universal LIFTED_CMC dominance over CRN.
- Output-level joint maximality or terminal/path optimality.
- A monotone temperature law or all-prompt direction.
- A semantic/GSM8K-quality certificate.
- Prompt-population or arbitrary-prompt generalization.
- Deployment-kernel, LLaDA, Dream, or new-theorem claims.
