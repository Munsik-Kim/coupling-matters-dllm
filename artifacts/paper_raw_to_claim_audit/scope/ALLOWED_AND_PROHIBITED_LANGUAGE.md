# Allowed and Prohibited Language

Allowed: certified fixed-suite or explicitly frozen prompt-population bounds under the implemented CMC; descriptive GSM8K; coordinatewise maximal coupling; simulated weight-only RTN; finite reachable-state lifted-interface audit.

Prohibited: joint/optimal coupling, arbitrary-prompt generalization, semantic-quality certificate, deployment-kernel claim, all-reachable-state lifted validation, or interpreting BF16 as FP32.
