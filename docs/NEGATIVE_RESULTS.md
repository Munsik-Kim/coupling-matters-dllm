# Preregistered negative results

## Additive token metrics

A frozen input-token-embedding hybrid metric and a tokenizer-byte metric
largely rescaled Hamming. They did not materially reduce independent-sample
saturation or improve descriptive GSM8K relevance.

## Terminal transport lower bound

Coordinatewise Kantorovich witnesses trained on a fixed suite transferred
poorly to the declared prompt population. Every formal simultaneous lower
confidence bound was zero under the preregistered protocol.

## Transport-weighted calibration

Survival-residual weighting changed clipping scales and improved some endpoint
fidelity and recovery diagnostics, especially at W3, but did not outperform
uniform clipping or RTN on the primary W4 GSM8K accuracy endpoint. Local
reconstruction error was a weak surrogate for split risk.
