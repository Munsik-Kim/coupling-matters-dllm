# Assumptions and scope

The principal certificate requires a fixed generation coordinate set, monotone MASK-to-token transitions, copy-only committed tokens, no remasking/editing, an explicit terminal kernel, and a weighted Hamming metric over generation positions only. CMC additionally requires recoverable factorized per-position transition laws.

Claims are limited to the specified models, samplers, fake weight-only RTN quantization, metrics, horizons, couplings, and declared prompt suites/populations. They are not semantic-quality, deployment-kernel, unseen-domain, or universal-optimality claims.
